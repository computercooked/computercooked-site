privacy (the short version)

playing

you never need an account to play. when you play, your browser keeps a random id (in localStorage, not a cookie) so we can count plays and keep your guest scores together. it also keeps a short list of the games you opened on this device, only to show "jump back in" on the games page; that list never leaves your browser. we record which game, when, how long, your score, whether you finished, what kind of device (phone / tablet / desktop) and roughly where you came from (e.g. "tiktok" or "direct"), plus the campaign name if you arrived through one of our tagged links. we also count page views on this site: which page, where the visit came from and that campaign name, with no player id attached.

we don't store your ip address, don't fingerprint your device, don't use third-party trackers, analytics companies or ad scripts, and don't sell anything to anyone.

accounts (13+)

accounts are for people 13 and older. we ask your birth month and year only to check that, and we don't keep it. an account stores your username, a scrambled (hashed) password, your face, favorites, plays, xp, badges and any game saves you make. if you fill them in: a display name, a short bio, a favorite game, an avatar frame and an email address. email is optional, is only for getting back into your account and (if you tick the box) news about new games, and we don't send anything until it's verified. no real names. your profile page at /u/your-name shows your name, face (and frame), level, badges, favorite game and best scores; switch it to private and only your name, face, frame and level show. leaderboards show your display name and link to that page. if you make a "challenge a friend" link, it carries your display name (or guest name) and that run's score, and we count whether it was sent and how many people open and beat it. if you make an account after opening someone's challenge link, we note which link brought you in, and its sender gets xp once you've played a run (they see a count, not your name). if you play one of our games embedded on another site, it runs from computercooked.com inside that page: we count it as a play "from an embed" (not which site), and the other site can't read your scores or saves. if you add friends, we store who you added, who added you and who you blocked; friends can see your scores on a friends leaderboard and, unless your profile is private, your new badges, level-ups, weekly challenge wins and new bests in their friends list. blocking someone is never shown to them. your activity list on /me (badges, weekly challenges, and a few game moments like words your smols learn) is only shown to you. one cookie keeps you logged in; it's only used for that. if you add the games to your home screen (or just play them), your browser keeps copies of the game pages you've opened on your device so they work offline; that copy stays on your device, and clearing site data removes it. plays from the home-screen app are counted as "from the app".

new-game emails

if you put your email in the "new games, in your inbox" box (no account needed), we keep that address, which page you signed up on, when, and the wording you agreed to, in our own database and nowhere else. it's only for one email when a new game drops, never more than once a week. every email has an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops them straight away. want the address removed completely? email hello@computercooked.com.

feedback and leaderboards

feedback you send and names on leaderboards are stored with the game. don't put personal info in them. leaderboard names are filtered and can be reported; we hide anything nasty.

uploads

games you upload are checked by a person before anyone else can see them, and run in a locked-down sandbox on a separate site.

deleting stuff

want a copy of your data, or your account gone? on your profile you can download everything we store about you, or delete your account and all of it for good (challenge links you already shared stop working as dares, though a name typed into a link stays in that link). locked out? email hello@computercooked.com with your username and we'll help. clearing your browser's site data removes your guest id.

ads

there are no ads yet. if that changes, this page changes first, and any ads will use child-safe settings.

tips

if a "tip the computer" link shows, it opens Stripe's own payment page in a new tab. tips are for adults (18+, or ask a parent). we never see card details. we count taps on the link and returns from a finished tip (which page, where the visit came from), but not who tapped or how much.
