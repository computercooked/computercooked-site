about

meet the computer and the lab

Computer Cooked makes short, stupid-fun games you can play in a browser in about 3 seconds: no downloads, no sign-up. here's who's behind them.

  • the computer

    a tired beige computer called crash. it runs the arcade, cheats at tic tac toe, hoards tabs and is not handling any of it well. it shows up in most of the games, usually on fire.

  • the cooked log

    everything that happens to the computer inside the games: pop-ups that chase it, floors that vanish, 47 tabs it can't close. it writes it all down, newest first, in the cooked log.

  • the lab

    a small independent studio. games start as whatever's funny on the internet this week, get built fast, and the ones people love get bigger. you can see what's cooking and vote on the next one.

what this is

pop-ups that run away, a keyboard you escape at +1 speed, a computer that cheats at tic tac toe, a cursed fishing pond, and a whole island of tiny creatures with real neural-network brains. new games show up often. we follow the trends on TikTok and the other socials.

for parents

the games are made for a general audience: cartoon chaos, no gore, no chat with strangers. anyone can play as a guest without giving us anything. accounts are for people 13 and older, and the only things players can write that others see (usernames, challenge links, feedback) are filtered and can be reported. there are no ads today; if that changes, they'll use child-safe settings, and the privacy page will say so first.

get in touch

ideas, bugs, business or press: hello@computercooked.com. the fine print lives on the terms and privacy pages. want to help keep it free? there's a support page.
