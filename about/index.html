about (the computer, explained)

what this is

Computer Cooked makes short, stupid-fun games you can play in a browser in about 3 seconds: no downloads, no sign-up. pop-ups that run away, a keyboard you escape at +1 speed, a computer that cheats at tic tac toe, a cursed fishing pond, and a whole island of tiny creatures with real neural-network brains. new games show up often, and the ones people love get bigger.

who makes it

a small independent studio, plus a tired beige computer that is not handling it well. the games come out of what's funny on the internet this week, and we follow the trends on TikTok and the other socials.

for parents

the games are made for a general audience: cartoon chaos, no gore, no chat with strangers. anyone can play as a guest without giving us anything. accounts are for people 13 and older, and the only things players can write that others see (usernames, challenge links, feedback) are filtered and can be reported. there are no ads today; if that changes, they'll use child-safe settings, and the privacy page will say so first.

get in touch

ideas, bugs, business or press: hello@computercooked.com. the fine print lives on the terms and privacy pages.
